## Idea

Send-this-to-her: a forwardable static for the flooded woman

## Rationale

The women's lane has a distribution problem no amount of targeting fixes: every impression is bought. The one format observed that manufactures free distribution is the post built to be sent to one named person - newme.asia shares at 0.40-0.49 on exactly that shape, against 0.03-0.06 on the same category's brand templates. Build one static whose entire job is to be forwarded: a short list of things the flooded woman has said out loud this month ('Everyone is the same', 'I closed the tab', 'I'm exhausted' - the language cues rules/targeting.md already records for this persona), set as separate floating lines around a single frame the way the @hitakkshi creative does, with Riteangle small at the bottom and no product claim in the type. The point is that she recognises a friend in it before she recognises an ad. Rs 1,000/day clears the Rs 800 funding floor that lrn-recorded weak reads keep tripping. Read it on the same LPV outcome as every other women's-lane test, not on shares - q-2026-08-28-does-meta-reward-shares is open precisely because the share signal may be unreadable on paid, and this test must not be designed to depend on it. Compliance: no man's photo, no money signal, no first-person claim in a synthetic voice - the copy is the audience's own words about themselves, not a testimonial about Riteangle.
