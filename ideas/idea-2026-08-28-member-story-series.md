## Idea

A named story series sourced from Riteangle's own members

## Rationale

Two independent accounts now run the same engine and it is the cheapest high-retention format in the reference set: ask the audience for the story, illustrate it, give the series a name. @induviduality has held an Indian women's audience on exactly Riteangle's subject matter for eight years on reader submissions alone, and Unshaadi's highest-engagement posts are other people's material plus a question. Riteangle has something neither of them has - members whose experience of the product is the story. The 2020 four-panel that reads closest to a Riteangle ad ('I am pretty, hot, financially independent... then why cannot he just love me?') was not briefed; it was sent in. Holding rather than recommending because the sourcing question is unanswered and it is not a creative question: nothing can be drafted until someone confirms a consented story exists. Note also that @induviduality itself is not a partnership candidate - half the observed posts are sexually explicit and none of it survives Meta's dating-vertical scrutiny - so this is the mechanic borrowed, not the creator approached.

## What would change the verdict

q-2026-08-28-first-party-consented-user-stories: whether a real, consented, first-person story from a Riteangle member can actually be obtained and published. Without one, rules/compliance.md 6.2 blocks the format outright - a synthetic person narrating a first-person Riteangle experience is a fabricated testimonial, and paraphrasing it into third person removes the only thing that makes the format work.
