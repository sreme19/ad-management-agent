## Idea

Ad copy positioning the replacement guarantee as a direct answer to paid-then-ignored complaints

## Rationale

Betterhalf's own live comment section documents the exact failure Riteangle's 'real match guaranteed, we replace a match that goes quiet' promise answers. That turns a generic trust claim into a checkable one.

## What would change the verdict

A compliance.md review of the copy. It sits close to two hard rules at once: no purchase language (compliance.md sec 2), and naming a competitor's paid tier invites a comparative claim we would have to substantiate. The research itself flagged it as subject to review before shipping, so calling it recommendable before that review has happened would be skipping the gate.
