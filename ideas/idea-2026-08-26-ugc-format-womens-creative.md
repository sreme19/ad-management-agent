## Idea

Test a UGC/testimonial-format women's creative against the produced-still format

## Rationale

Creator/UGC is one of only two formats that beat the category's engagement floor, and Plenty of Fish's testimonial-to-camera reels show the format works without a paid creator. This tests the format lesson using a produced asset, so it needs no creator fee and clears budget.md's floor on its own. Same audience and destination as WOMEN_18-22_CASUAL_LPV, so the format is the only variable - which is what creative-generation.md sec 8 requires. Still bound by the POV rule: a testimonial from her point of view, not a woman shown as the object of the frame.
