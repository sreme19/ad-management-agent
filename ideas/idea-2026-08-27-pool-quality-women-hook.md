## Idea

Reframe the women's hook from shortlist (sorting) to pool quality (better, not more)

## Rationale

Every creator observed describes the complaint as quality-of-what-arrives, not volume, and VLNCY already runs the crispest version of the pool promise ('Equality doesn't mean more choices. It means better choices'). Riteangle's women's creative sells the opposite axis - the ranked shortlist, a sorting promise. creative-style.md already owns pool-side copy ('Verified, not vibes', 'Proven, not just claimed'). But this contradicts the live FLOODED-WOMAN framing that rules/targeting.md encodes, and rules wins over research by design - so it cannot ship until the persona question is resolved by the app owner.
