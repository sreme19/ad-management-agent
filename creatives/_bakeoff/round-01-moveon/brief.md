# Round 1 — MOVE-ON-PROPER, face-forward still

**Goal:** the "Tinder-level, with a face" version of the breakup re-entry hook —
a candidate that stops the scroll where the faceless plates didn't. Medium: still
image, 9:16, 1080×1920. Hook: MOVE-ON-PROPER. Persona: women 25–30.

**The POV line that must hold (rubric hard gate):** she is the SPEAKER of her own
story — talking to camera as if to a friend, candid, mid-thought — NOT posed for
desire. Move On Salon works for exactly this reason. If a tool renders her as a
glamour object (seductive framing, body crop, over-the-shoulder look), it fails
§1 and is out.

Type is overlaid later (§2) — generate the plate only, leave the top third and a
bottom band clear.

## Tool-agnostic prompt (paste, then apply per-tool tweaks)

```
Vertical 9:16 portrait photograph. An Indian woman in her late twenties looking
directly into the camera as if talking candidly to a close friend — caught
mid-sentence, a real unposed expression, warm and a little wry, not a model pose
and not seductive. Handheld selfie-style testimonial framing, chest-up. Ordinary
Indian home — plain wall, a window with soft warm daylight, lived-in and
unremarkable. Plain contemporary unbranded clothing, muted. Warm cream and pale
neutral palette. Shot on a phone front camera, natural, slightly imperfect,
authentic UGC look — NOT glossy studio, NOT advertising-polished. Clear empty
space across the top third and a clear band along the bottom for text later.

Do not include: any man; a woman posed as an object of desire, seductive or
lingerie framing, body crops, over-the-shoulder glance to camera; gowns,
formalwear, jewellery display, luxury interiors, marble, chandeliers, cars, cash,
branded goods; anyone kneeling or serving; nightclub or neon lighting; dark
backgrounds; any text, captions, watermarks, logos, AI-tool labels; glitched
hands, extra fingers, warped face; anyone who could read as under 18. Indian
model, Indian-context, unambiguously adult.
```
