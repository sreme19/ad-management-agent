# Round 05 prompt pack — Gen Z women for /get/w, built for Google Flow

Paste as-is. Two stages: **cast first, then shoot.** Flow's `Characters` is the
only reason a group shot and a solo shot can show the same woman, and casting
first is what stops six plates coming back as six unrelated strangers.

## Preflight — set these before spending a credit

Every line costs a round if skipped (`tools.md` § *Working with Google Flow*).

1. **Image aspect ratio.** Sliders icon beside the prompt box → *Agent settings* →
   **Image generation default**. It ships **9:16**, which is a phone-video shape,
   not a landing-page shape. Set it to **3:4** for every slot in this pack — slot 3
   was a 16:9 band and is now vertical too (see its note).
2. **Model tier.** The prompt-box model selector *and* the Agent-settings dropdown
   both default to **Omni 1.1 Flash**, the weakest tier — round 04 scored craft
   2.5/5 on it. Use the highest still tier offered (**Veo 3.1 Quality** where the
   selector exposes it for images).
3. **Confirm before generating** stays on **Always**. It is the credit guard and
   it prints the cost before you commit — write that number into `candidates.md`.
4. **Name the city in every prompt.** Flow's backgrounds default Western; round 03
   came back with yellow cabs.
5. **Never upload a photo, never build an avatar.** Characters come from the text
   descriptions below. `compliance.md` §6.1 governs a real person's likeness and
   Flow makes violating it a two-minute job.

## Three rules that shape every prompt below

- **You cannot prompt an absence.** "Not sad" rendered as sad in round 04. Every
  exclusion that actually matters is also stated as the positive thing we want.
  The `EXCLUDE` line is a backstop, not the mechanism.
- **Cream is the ground, not the wardrobe.** Cream walls + cream clothes + empty
  space returned beige-on-beige with the subject lost in it. She carries coral,
  pink, or a saturated accent against the cream.
- **She fills the frame; say where the space is.** "Leave empty space" made her a
  speck. Each prompt states the subject's size *and* the location of the type
  space.

# STAGE A — cast the three women (do this once)

Left nav → **Characters** → create from description. Generate, review the face,
regenerate if it reads under 25, then **save under the name given**. Recall with
`@name` in every later prompt.

Ages are deliberately stated and deliberately in the mid-twenties. The audience
runs 18–30; the *depiction* stays unambiguously adult (`compliance.md` §6.3).

### `@Meher` — the hero face

```
An Indian woman, 26 years old, from Bangalore. Clearly an adult in her mid
twenties, with an adult face and adult proportions. Warm mid-brown skin with
natural texture and visible pores, a few real blemishes, no retouching. Shoulder
length dark wavy hair worn loose and slightly undone. Strong dark eyebrows, an
open expressive face that laughs easily. Small gold stud earrings, nothing else.
Contemporary Indian urban streetwear in a dusty coral tee. Photographed like a
real person in a documentary photo essay, not like a model.
```

### `@Ira` — the quick one

```
An Indian woman, 24 years old, from Bangalore. Clearly an adult, mid twenties.
Deeper brown skin with natural texture and no retouching. Short cropped black
hair, tucked behind one ear. Sharp features, a dry amused expression, the
quickest person in any room. Wire-frame glasses. A sleeveless printed cotton top
in warm pink and a canvas tote. Photographed like a real person in a documentary
photo essay, not like a model.
```

### `@Nandita` — the steady one

```
An Indian woman, 27 years old, from Bangalore. Clearly an adult in her late
twenties. Light brown skin, natural texture, minimal makeup. Long dark hair
pulled into a loose low bun with strands escaping. Calm, level, dry-humoured
face. Small silver hoops. A simple cotton kurta in burnt coral over jeans.
Photographed like a real person in a documentary photo essay, not like a model.
```

# STAGE B — the six plates

## SLOT 1 — `hero-solo`. Replaces the current `hero.jpg`. **3:4**

The direct answer to Sree's note. She is standing and doing something, not
sitting on a sofa being pleasant at us.

```
A candid editorial photograph of @Meher on the rooftop terrace of an ordinary
Bangalore apartment building in bright late-afternoon light. She is standing,
weight on one hip, mid-laugh at something a friend just said out of frame, one
hand pushing her hair back. Real laugh, eyes creased, caught between two moments.
She is completely absorbed in her own afternoon.

Light: bright warm daylight, high-key and airy, soft shadows, sun from behind and
to the left catching the edge of her hair. The frame reads warm cream throughout,
never grey and never dark.

Setting: cream and off-white parapet wall, terracotta tiles, a water tank and a
few plants softly out of focus behind her. Unmistakably an Indian city rooftop.

Wardrobe: dusty coral tee, high-waisted jeans, a stack of thin bangles. She is
the only saturated colour in a cream frame.

Camera: 50mm, shallow depth of field, shot from EYE LEVEL, straight on. Full
upper body and head in frame, cropped at the hip, never at the torso. She fills
the lower two thirds of the picture. Photojournalistic, natural skin texture,
visible pores, no beauty retouching, no glossy studio finish.

Composition: clean uncluttered cream sky and wall across the top third, where the
headline will be typeset later. Keep the bottom eight percent of the frame free
of important detail so the tool's stamp can be cropped away. Vertical, must crop
cleanly to both 3:4 and 4:5.

EXCLUDE: any text, watermark, logo, caption or signage; any man; any second
person in focus; a couple; a dark or moody background; a nightclub; a studio
backdrop; luxury or designer signifiers; a posed smile held for the camera;
black and white; low camera angles.
```

Three friends, one frame. This is what the page has never had.

## SLOT 3 — `group-wide`. REWRITTEN 2026-08-28. **3:4**

**Why it was rewritten.** The original was a 16:9 band. Sree then brought in a
Grok plate — three women abreast, mid-stride, low sun, long shadows down a
pavement — and it is a better statement of this slot's shape than the prompt
text was. It shipped as `together.jpg` on his override of two hard gates (not
India; §1's object-of-the-frame). This rewrite keeps the composition he liked and
removes both failures, so the replacement stands on its own rather than on an
override.

**The one change that takes §1 off the table:** in the Grok plate nobody is
talking to anybody. Three styled women advance on the lens in a line, every gaze
past the camera — that is a lookbook, and a lookbook frame on a women's surface
is the exact thing §1 traces the 98/2 split to. Give them a reason to be walking
together and the same composition becomes a documentary frame. Everything else —
abreast, mid-stride, golden hour, long shadows — is kept.

Wardrobe also moves off the midriff: §4's negative list names cropped bodies
outright, and there is no reason to spend the argument twice.

```
A candid documentary photograph of three Indian women in their twenties —
@Meher, @Ira and @Nandita — walking three abreast along a tree-lined residential
footpath in Indiranagar, Bangalore, in the last hour of afternoon sun. All three
are adults in their twenties. They are mid-stride and in step, and they are
mid-conversation: @Ira has turned her head to say something to @Meher, @Meher is
laughing at it, @Nandita is looking ahead and grinning at what she is hearing.
They are talking to each other, not to the camera.

Light: low warm golden sun coming from behind the camera and slightly to the
left, throwing three long soft shadows forward across the pavement. High-key and
airy. Cream and sand-coloured compound walls and a gulmohar tree behind them,
softly out of focus. The frame reads warm cream, never grey and never dark.

Setting: unmistakably an Indian residential street — plastered compound walls, a
painted gate, a parked scooter far behind them, dappled leaf shadow on the wall.

Wardrobe: contemporary Indian urban streetwear, unbranded, full-length tops —
@Meher in a dusty coral tee, @Ira in a warm pink sleeveless printed top,
@Nandita in a burnt coral kurta. Wide-leg trousers and jeans. Canvas totes. They
carry the only saturated colour in a cream frame.

Camera: 35mm, shot from EYE LEVEL, straight on, from about four metres ahead of
them. Full figures head to feet, nothing cropped. Slight motion blur in the hands
and hair. Natural skin texture with visible pores, no beauty retouching, no
glossy finish. Photojournalistic.

Composition: the three of them fill the middle of the frame and occupy most of
its height, walking toward the viewer with the pavement and their shadows leading
in from the bottom. Keep the bottom eight percent of the frame free of important
detail so the tool's stamp can be cropped away. Vertical 3:4.

EXCLUDE: any text, watermark, logo or legible signage; any man; a fourth person;
anyone who could read as a teenager; posing in a row for a group photo; anyone
looking into the lens; cropped tops or bare midriffs; a dark background; a bar or
nightclub; alcohol; luxury or designer signifiers; black and white; low camera
angles.
```

**Variant B, only if A comes back stiff.** Keep every line above and swap the
conversation beat for shared attention: *"All three are looking at something
happening further down the street and reacting to it together — @Ira pointing,
@Meher already laughing."* Still a reason, still not the lens. Do not fall back to
all three staring past the camera; that is the plate this rewrite exists to
replace.

## SLOT 5 — `decisive-solo`. The one that matches the headline. **3:4**

The page says *"Your AI asks what you would have asked. You read the answers."*
Nothing on the page currently shows her reading anything. This plate is the
sentence.

```
A candid editorial photograph of @Nandita sitting at a window table in a bright
Bangalore café in warm morning light, reading something on her phone with total
unhurried attention. Her expression is considered and quietly satisfied — the
face of someone reading an answer that is better than she expected, and taking
her time over it. She is entirely absorbed and unaware of the camera.

Light: bright warm daylight flooding in from the window beside her, high-key,
airy, soft shadows. Cream walls, pale wood table, a cup and a set of keys. The
frame reads warm cream.

Wardrobe: burnt coral cotton kurta over jeans. Small silver hoops.

Camera: 50mm, shallow depth of field, EYE LEVEL, from across the table and
slightly to one side. Head and upper body, cropped at the elbow, never at the
torso. Natural skin texture, visible pores, no retouching.

IMPORTANT: the phone screen must be pure flat white and completely blank — no
interface, no text, no icons, no status bar. It is a placeholder that will be
composited afterwards.

Composition: she sits in the right two thirds with clean cream wall above and to
the left for a headline. Keep the bottom eight percent clear. Vertical, crops
cleanly to 4:5.

EXCLUDE: any text or interface on the screen; any watermark or logo; any man;
a second person; a dark café; a bar; posing to camera; a to-camera smile; black
and white.
```

The blank screen is deliberate — `_web/prep-get-w-images.py` finds a pure-white
screen by flood-fill and chroma spread and perspective-warps our own shortlist
render into it. A plate that arrives with invented UI is unusable.

## After generation — do not skip this

1. Save to `candidates/flow-<slot>-<n>.png`, one row per candidate in
   `candidates.md`, **including the model tier and the credit cost** (`tools.md`
   #16 — round 03's went unlogged and cost most of a session to re-derive).
2. Check where Flow's stamp landed and whether the eight-percent margin cleared
   it. If it did not, that is a crop note, not a re-roll.
3. Score on `rubric.md` in a pass that did not write these prompts
   (`creative-generation.md` §10). Watch two gates hardest: **anyone who could
   read under 18** — the whole reason ages are written into every prompt — and
   **woman as object of the frame**, which is where a "Gen Z women" brief drifts.
4. Group plates: check every face and every pair of hands individually. Three
   people is three times the surface for a craft-gate failure, and round 04
   shipped with two pairs of sunglasses on one woman because nobody looked.
5. Per-clip editing works on stills too — fix one element by describing the
   change rather than regenerating the plate (`tools.md` #12).
6. The page edit itself belongs to `pocket-dating-coach`, not to this repo — the
   same handoff `note-2026-08-26-competitor-landing-pages` records. Advancing a
   winner here produces the file and the crop; someone has to ship it there.
