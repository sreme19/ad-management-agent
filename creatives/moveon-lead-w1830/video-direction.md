# Video direction — the ENERGY-DRAIN cut, women 18–30, Meta

Captured 2026-08-28 from the app owner in session. Not yet a prompt pack — his own Grok/Flow
prompts are to be the starting register, per his note that the in-repo bakeoff prompts are weaker
than what he has been using. **Ask for those before writing prompts.**

## Format

- **~20 seconds, assembled from 3–4 clips of ~6s each**, generated separately in Google Flow and
  cut together. Six seconds alone is too short to carry the drain-list structure.
- **No dialogue. No spoken lines at all.** Music/beat-driven.
- **Every word is on-screen type**, overlaid in the edit — never generated into the footage.
- **Hinglish**, romanised, with some Hindi words carried in.
- **Multiple women. No man in frame at any point.**

## Why no dialogue is the important decision

`compliance.md` §6.2 blocks a generated person from narrating a first-person experience of using
Riteangle. That is what blocked the MOVE-ON *video* while clearing the still — its 7–12s line was
exactly the prohibited form, and the rule names the fix: "rewriting those lines out of first person,
unblocks it."

**A silent cut has no first person to rewrite.** The complaint and the payoff both live in type,
which is brand voice. This is not a workaround; it is the remedy the rule itself specifies.

Second benefit, unrelated to compliance: **most Meta feed and Reels video is watched muted.** A cut
that carries its whole argument in type is not damaged by a muted view — it is designed for it.

## The trap that more women in frame does NOT solve

`creative-generation.md` §1 is not satisfied by removing the man. **Three styled women advancing on
the lens is a lookbook, and a lookbook on a women's surface is precisely what §1 traces the 98/2
split to.** This is not theoretical — it is what happened on round-05 slot 3: a Grok plate of three
women abreast, every gaze past the camera, shipped on an override and then rewritten specifically to
remove it.

The rewrite's own diagnosis is the rule to carry into every clip here:

> in the Grok plate nobody is talking to anybody … Give them a reason to be walking together and the
> same composition becomes a documentary frame.

**So: more women is more surface for this failure, not less.** Every clip needs the women absorbed in
each other or in their own moment. Nobody looks into the lens. Nobody is presented.

## What the clips have to carry

The structure is the drain-list, then the flip:

| clip | beat | on-screen type (draft — needs a native-speaker pass) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the drain | `Ghosting.` |
| 2 | the drain | `Fake profiles.` |
| 3 | the drain | `Teen hafte ki texting jo kahin nahi jaati.` |
| 4 | the turn | `Ab samajh aaya energy kahan jaati hai?` |
| 5 | the flip | `Date mat karo. Bas milo, aur jiyo.` |
| — | CTA | `Apply karo →` |

Alternate opener under consideration: **`Swipe karte karte saal nikal gaya.`**

The drain beats read as *her lived experience*, never as an accusation about a named competitor —
that distinction is a platform-policy and defamation boundary, not a stylistic preference.

**The one-night-stand drain was cut deliberately.** `targeting.md` defines the target persona
CASUAL-SELECTIVE as wanting "physical + verified lifestyle/social-currency signal without labels".
An ad attacking casual intent contradicts the segment being sold to.

## Production notes

1. **Casting is already done and is reusable.** `creatives/_bakeoff/round-05-getw-genz/prompts.md`
   Stage A defines `@Meher`, `@Ira` and `@Nandita` as saved Flow Characters. That pack's own note —
   "Flow's `Characters` is the only reason a group shot and a solo shot can show the same woman" — is
   exactly what a multi-clip cut needs, and it is the reason 3–4 separately generated clips can show
   the same people. **Reuse the casting; do not reuse the plate prompts** (those are 3:4 page
   imagery).
2. **Aspect: 9:16, 1080×1920** for Reels/Stories. Round-05's preflight sets Flow to 3:4 for the page —
   wrong for this. Change it before spending credits.
3. **Safe zones.** Reels and Stories UI obscures roughly the top 14% and bottom 20%. All type lands
   between. A headline that tests fine on desktop can sit under the caption bar on a phone.
4. **Type is overlaid, never prompted.** `creative-generation.md` §2. Doubly true for Hindi — image
   and video models garble Latin text and are far worse with Devanagari.
5. **Fonts.** Gabarito carries the lowercase `riteangle` mark and is Latin-only. Devanagari needs a
   paired face — Mukta or Noto Sans Devanagari sit well with it.
6. **Music rights.** A commercial track is a licensing problem on a paid ad. Use Meta's own Sound
   Collection or Flow-generated audio. Confirm before the cut is finalised.
7. **Craft gate, per round-05's own after-generation checklist:** with several women per frame across
   four clips, check every face and every pair of hands individually. Round 04 shipped two pairs of
   sunglasses on one woman because nobody looked.
