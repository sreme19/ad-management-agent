## Claim

Brand-voice organic content earns nothing in this category; only creator/UGC and one produced brand film break the pattern

## Evidence

- (2026-08-26) Engagement rate vs followers across ~17 accounts: Shaadi 0.016%, BharatMatrimony 0.007%, Coffee Meets Bagel 0.025%, Happn 0.017%, QuackQuack 0.027%, Bumble brand carousel 0.10%. Against that: Bumble x @ahillyeah creator reel ~2% on the creator's base (11.6K likes), Aisle's produced brand film 3.9% (6,238 on 158K). BharatMatrimony posting 4,892 times for single-digit likes is volume without a format that works.
- (2026-08-27) **supported**: 27 Aug 2026 scanning session, same platform and category, same week. Phone-shot bedroom talking-heads: @anerithakkar_ 5,499 likes, @malikkaadvani 3,229, @wingwoman.sim 1,657, @aseemtx 5,911. Produced brand assets: shaadi.com Raksha Bandhan 31 likes, VLNCY 'better choices' 17, VLNCY Friendship Day reel 4, VLNCY World Cup 3. Follower counts differ and none of this is normalised, so it is not an engagement-rate claim — but the split between the two groups is not marginal. Adds two accounts (VLNCY, malaikaneri) the original sweep never saw. See note-2026-08-27-three-women-creators-on-dating.
