## Claim

Brand-voice organic content earns nothing in this category; only creator/UGC and one produced brand film break the pattern

## Evidence

- (2026-08-26) Engagement rate vs followers across ~17 accounts: Shaadi 0.016%, BharatMatrimony 0.007%, Coffee Meets Bagel 0.025%, Happn 0.017%, QuackQuack 0.027%, Bumble brand carousel 0.10%. Against that: Bumble x @ahillyeah creator reel ~2% on the creator's base (11.6K likes), Aisle's produced brand film 3.9% (6,238 on 158K). BharatMatrimony posting 4,892 times for single-digit likes is volume without a format that works.
