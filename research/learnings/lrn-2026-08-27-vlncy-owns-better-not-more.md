## Claim

A Bangalore competitor already owns the pool-quality promise in its crispest form, and states it as the exact opposite of the volume framing Riteangle's women's creative is built on

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) VLNCY runs 'Equality doesn't mean more choices. It means better choices.' as overlay copy, with '50:50 men-to-women ratio, guaranteed' as the caption claim on two of three observed creatives, plus 'compatibility-based matching' and the tagline DATE WITHOUT SURPRISES. Across all three assets there is no shortlist, ranking, or AI-does-the-work claim — the promise is entirely about who is in the pool. Riteangle's women's lane sells the opposite axis: rules/creative-style.md's 'A shortlist that means something' and 'We do the matching. You skip the work.' answer sorting effort, not pool quality. VLNCY was absent from the 24-row matrix in note-2026-08-26-competitor-instagram-sweep, so this positioning was not on the board when the women's creative was written.
