## Claim

Animating from a frame that already shows the end of an action produces a clip that holds that state for its full duration, so storyboard start frames must show the beginning of the action

## Evidence

- (2026-08-30) Riya's Act 1 beat was 'hand rises to cover her mouth as the realisation lands'. The Flow start frame already had her hand at her mouth, and the resulting clip held that pose for all four seconds so the moment of realisation never happened. Flow's own agent independently reached the same diagnosis when asked. Regenerating a corrected start frame with both hands down on the table, then animating that, produced the hand rising at the midpoint. The same failure mode explains why Act 2 could not be animated from the Act 2 frames, which already showed her head up in warm light and so left nothing to occur. CONSEQUENCE FOR PLANNING: brief storyboard frames as the first moment of a beat, not the iconic moment. The iconic moment is what the clip should ARRIVE at.
