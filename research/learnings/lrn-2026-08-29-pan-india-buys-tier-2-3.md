## Claim

Pan-India targeting at a low daily budget concentrates delivery in cheaper Tier-2 and Tier-3 geographies rather than metros, because Meta buys the cheapest impressions inside whatever geography it is given

## Evidence

- (2026-08-29) Indian benchmark sources report CPM varying sharply by geography inside India — ads to South Delhi or South Mumbai cost much more than Tier-3 targeting in Bihar or Uttar Pradesh, with India CPM spanning roughly Rs 50-400. A budget-constrained ad set given the whole country will spend where impressions are cheapest. The MOVE-ON funnel went pan-India on 2026-08-28 at Rs 300/day per ad set, so delivery should be expected to land outside the metros. TWO CONSEQUENCES THAT PULL OPPOSITE WAYS. It is coherent with the Hinglish decision — Hindi-first copy fits a Hindi-belt audience far better than it fits Bangalore, which is Kannada-speaking, and that tension is exactly why pan-India was chosen. It is NOT coherent with the creative: the round-05 casting in creatives/_bakeoff/round-05-getw-genz/prompts.md is written around Bangalore rooftops, Indiranagar footpaths and cafe window tables, and names the city in every prompt because Flow's backgrounds default Western. So the copy is being aimed at one audience and the imagery at another.
