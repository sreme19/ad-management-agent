## Claim

Two rival dating brands are running blush-pun billboards with cosmetics partners in the same window, which makes the beauty category the contested co-brand territory in Indian dating

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Shaadi.com x MARS Cosmetics ran 'Blush chahiye? / Ya blush karane wala?' on an NCR billboard, posted ~20 Aug 2026 and taking 20.2K likes as a collab post. Tinder India is running a jigsaw-reveal creative captioned 'Swipe to see who we are flirting with' over a photographed roadside billboard reading 'Blush?' with the NYKAA wordmark — Nykaa being India's largest beauty retailer. Same word, same medium, same category of partner, two rivals, one observation window. Shaadi resolves the pun in Hinglish; Tinder keeps it English and withholds the partner for the swipe. Neither cost, reach nor conversion is known, and it cannot be told from this whether one is answering the other. Bears on lrn-2026-08-27-cobranding-is-the-default-unit: the partner category is not arbitrary, and beauty is where two of the biggest players landed independently.
