## Claim

The gender-ratio guarantee is a claim Riteangle cannot answer in kind, because its own membership ratio is the thing the claim would expose

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) VLNCY states 'guaranteed 50:50 gender ratio' as its headline caption claim. rules/creative-style.md records Riteangle's own membership at 31 men to 17 women, and the same file derives the median-woman-has-14-suitors figure from exactly that imbalance. So the flood the women's creative is built on and the ratio VLNCY attacks are the same fact seen from two sides. Any Riteangle move onto pool-quality ground has to find a formulation that does not invite the ratio comparison, and rules/compliance.md forbids unverified claims, which rules out matching the guarantee rhetorically without the numbers to back it.
