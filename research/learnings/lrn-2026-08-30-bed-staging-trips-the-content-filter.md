## Claim

A woman positioned on a bed at night reliably trips the image content filter even in fully-clothed non-sexual staging, and concealment verbs describing a partner also trip it; both have passing alternatives with the same emotional read

## Evidence

- (2026-08-30) Meera's Act 1 frame, sitting on the edge of an unmade bed at night lit by her phone, was blocked as a possible policy violation across repeated attempts with varied wording. Restaging her sitting on the floor with her back against the side of the bed, fully dressed in t-shirt and pyjama trousers and framed from the chest up, passed first time and produced a stronger frame. Separately, describing a man tilting his phone away to hide it was blocked in the restaurant scene; looking down at his own phone with his face turned away passed and read the same. CONSEQUENCE FOR PLANNING: for the loneliness-at-night beat, brief floor-beside-the-bed. Avoid concealment verbs when describing a partner's behaviour.
