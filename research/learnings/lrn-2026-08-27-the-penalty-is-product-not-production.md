## Claim

The engagement penalty in this category attaches to whether a post is about the product, not to who is speaking or how it is made

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Rushi Ahuja of Unshaadi posts both kinds from one account in the same format seven weeks apart. His patriarchy-commentary reel 'Where is the bar?', which never names the business, took 5,564 likes and 135 comments on 4 Aug 2026. His reel explaining Unshaadi's registration process and its refusal of the income filter took 138 likes and 64 comments on 17 Jun 2026. Same face, same phone, same length — 40x apart on the single variable of whether the post is about the product. This sharpens lrn-2026-08-26-brand-voice-organic-is-dead, which attributed the penalty to brand-voice production. It is not production: it is subject matter. Note the counterweight in the comment ratio — the product reel drew 64 comments on 138 likes (0.46) against the commentary reel's 135 on 5,564 (0.02), so the product post converted attention into argument at a far higher rate on much less reach.
- (2026-08-28) **supported**: (2026-08-28) Two Indian fashion retailers, each compared only against itself. nykaafashion runs a repeating 'WHAT'S NEW on NYKAA FASHION - <brand>' template across at least three posts (Birkenstock 124 likes, max 177, Squatwolf 90) and its one post with no template, no partner logo and no product name in the type - 'back to shopping because healing was getting expensive' - takes 576, three to six times the template, on the same account in the same period. newme.asia's most-commented post in a four-post set is the one that lists five things a man did ('He never posts me', 'He's a red flag') and never names the garment: 148 comments against 12 on the account's 18.1K-like winner. And the sharpest version: heynnnow posted a studio 'Spoil your sibling edit' poster for 1 like the same week newme.asia posted 'ask your sibling what's your vibe' for 2,895 likes and 1,154 shares. Same festival, same country, same customer - one depicts the occasion, the other gives the viewer an errand. Unnormalised counts, single screenshots, boosting not ruled out.
