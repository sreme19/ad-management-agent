## Claim

Grok Imagine can perform a full two-state transition of posture, expression and colour grade together from a single start frame when the prompt describes it as one continuous transformation, so frame-to-frame interpolation is not required for a turning-point shot

## Evidence

- (2026-08-30) The Riteangle cut's hinge required each woman to lift her head from defeat to resolve while the light flipped from cool blue-grey to warm gold. Google Flow supports start-frame/end-frame interpolation and was set up to do it. Testing Grok from the Act 1 frame alone first, the transition landed in one clip: measured mean R minus B across the 6s went from -17.7 to +104.0 and luma from 32.0 to 113.5, with the phone lowering and the head lifting in the same move. Repeated successfully for all four women, end values +94, +84 and +116. CONSEQUENCE FOR PLANNING: a grade-flip turn does not need two engines or two frames, which keeps a whole ad inside one animation engine.
