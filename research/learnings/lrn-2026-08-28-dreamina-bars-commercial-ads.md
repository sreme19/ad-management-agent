## Claim

Dreamina, the easiest route to Seedance, forbids commercial advertising in its own terms and takes a perpetual sublicensable licence over what you generate

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) Dreamina/CapCut terms read 28 Aug 2026: the services are 'generally provided for private, non-commercial use', and users may not 'use the Services to advertise or perform any commercial solicitation'. The user retains ownership of outputs but grants ByteDance a perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable licence over assets uploaded or generated. dreamina.capcut.com itself returned HTTP 200, exposing Seedance 2.5, 2.0 and 2.0-mini with free daily credits, while capcut.com/create returned HTTP 451 Unavailable For Legal Reasons - so reachability is uneven and not a guide to permission. By contrast fal.ai carries an explicit commercial-use badge on Seedance 1.5 Pro, 2.0 and 2.5, restricts only US-embargoed countries so India is eligible, and bills prepaid USD credits by card or ACH, which sidesteps the RBI recurring-mandate problem that breaks international subscriptions. Replicate's terms are stronger still, granting the user all right title and interest in output explicitly for commercial purposes. Why this matters here: the SSD Scale Up Meeting of 6 Aug 2026 records the ads team using an unnamed 'third-party tool' for Seedance with a sub-2,000-character prompt limit, which fits Dreamina's consumer surface. If that is the tool, the assets it produced are outside its licence for advertising use. Also verified and relevant to India: Seedance lip-sync covers English, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Cantonese and Sichuanese, and does not cover Hindi.
