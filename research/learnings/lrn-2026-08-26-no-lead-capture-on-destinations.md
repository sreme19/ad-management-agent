## Claim

Riteangle's paid destinations capture no contactable lead, where every mass-market Indian competitor captures one above the fold

## Evidence

- (2026-08-26) /get and /get/w carry one CTA repeated three times - a Play Store link - and no email or phone field. TrulyMadly and BharatMatrimony both put OTP phone capture in the hero; Shaadi.com puts a four-dropdown preference picker there as a foot-in-the-door before asking for contact details. Three independent players converging on the same pattern. Aisle is the one competitor with the same gap as us, and its funnel is the one not to copy.
- (2026-08-27) **partly closed**: /get/w now captures a WhatsApp number or an email and writes it to `marketing_leads`, the table that had existed since 2026-08-15 with a dialer and an email drip attached and had never been written to by any code. The Play tap stays the primary CTA and the form sits under it rather than in front of it, so this is an addition to the funnel and not a gate across it. The claim still holds for /get, which captures nothing. Whether the form converts or merely adds friction is q-2026-08-26-lead-capture-conversion and is not answered by building it.
- (2026-08-27) **supported**: 27 Aug 2026: @malaikaneri, a professional Indian matchmaker, captures contactable leads in the comment section of an organic reel via a 'Comment PROFILES' keyword instruction — 101 comments on 142 likes. This extends the original finding: the gap is not only that Riteangle's paid destinations capture no lead, but that a competitor is capturing them upstream of any paid destination at all. See note-2026-08-27-malaikaneri-comment-to-dm-funnel.
