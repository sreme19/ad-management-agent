## Claim

Truecaller for Business (business.truecaller.com) does not sell audience advertising and is the wrong funnel to enter — its products are Verified Caller ID, Verified Messages, Verified Campaigns, Business Chat, Dialing/Number Intelligence and User Authentication, with advertising absent from the menu entirely.

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Opened the Products menu on business.truecaller.com on 2026-08-27 and read all three product groups; /advertising returns 404. Advertising lives on the separate adsmanager.truecaller.com / advertisers.truecaller.com properties.
