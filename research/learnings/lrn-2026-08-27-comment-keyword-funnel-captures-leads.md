## Claim

A comment-keyword DM funnel captures contactable leads on an organic post at zero media cost, which is the same gap as the landing-page finding but one layer earlier in the funnel

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) @malaikaneri's post carries the caption instruction 'Comment PROFILES to see the 101 most attractive men in my database' and shows 101 comments against 142 likes — a 0.71 comment-to-like ratio. Every other post observed the same day sits between 0.007 and 0.02. That shape is a keyword funnel, not a discussion. It converts an organic post into a lead-capture form with no ad spend behind it. lrn-2026-08-26-no-lead-capture-on-destinations records the same absence on Riteangle's paid destinations; this shows a competitor solving it before the click exists. The DM automation itself was inferred from the caption plus the ratio, not observed, and the comments were not read.
- (2026-08-28) **inconclusive**: (2026-08-28) The mechanic is running on a verified retail brand, not only on matchmakers - newme.asia's Tomato Red carousel captions 'Comment Colours to grab the prettiest fits of the season!' - but it does not reproduce the ratio the claim rests on. 89 comments against 2,028 likes is 0.044, against malaikaneri's 0.71. So the mechanic travels and the shape does not travel with it. Either the ask has to be worth something specific (malaikaneri offered a named list of 101 people; newme offered a coupon-flavoured 'grab'), or a large retail follower base dilutes it. Neither can be told apart from one screenshot. See note-2026-08-28-fashion-feed-format-split.
