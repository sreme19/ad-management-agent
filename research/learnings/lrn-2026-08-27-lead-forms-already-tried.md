## Claim

On-platform lead forms are not an untried option for women — they already ran and produced a 98% male submission mix, and this repo's recorded diagnosis blames creative POV and submit-optimised delivery, not the lead-form format

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) The operator described capturing leads on the ad platform as something 'which has been tried in the past' (note-2026-08-27-womens-funnel-matrix) without stating the outcome. The outcome is already recorded here three times: rules/creative-generation.md:5 names the four live assets behind it, including RA_LEAD_WOMEN_SNAP1_20260818 — a women-targeted lead asset — and reports 98% of lead-form submissions were men and 100% of /get Play Store taps were men. rules/destinations.yaml:9 and SPEC.md:120 cite the same result as the origin of the destination-audience gate. The diagnosis in creative-generation.md section 1 is explicit that both failures were generation-time: three of the four assets put a woman in the frame as the object of it, and 'on a submit-optimized objective the delivery algorithm compounds it, because men submit dating lead forms far more readily.' That attributes the skew to POV plus objective, and leaves the format itself unjudged. Two things have changed since: the POV rule now exists as a rule, and /get/w exists as a women-framed destination (registered 2026-08-26). Filed own-research rather than live-data because no sample size or date range is recorded anywhere for the 98/2 figure — see the question raised alongside this.
