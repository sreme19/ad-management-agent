## Claim

Bumble's fatigue remedy is framed as reducing overwhelm and choice volume, which is the axis Riteangle's own creator research already identified as the wrong diagnosis

## Evidence

- (2026-08-29) Bumble's reported remedy is removing the swipe and serving users who 'crave an experience that feels less overwhelming and more purposeful' — an answer to too-much-choice. lrn-2026-08-27-complaint-is-quality-not-volume found the opposite across five creators: not one of them described too many options to sort; every complaint was about the worth of the attention arriving. lrn-2026-08-27-vlncy-owns-better-not-more found a Bangalore competitor stating it outright — 'Equality doesn't mean more choices. It means better choices.' So the incumbent is occupying the fatigue territory while answering it on the volume axis, and two independent strands of this repo's own research say that axis is misdiagnosed. That is the differentiation available to the MOVE-ON drain-list: ghosting, fake profiles and texting-that-goes-nowhere are all QUALITY complaints, not volume complaints, so the drain-list is on the axis Bumble is not addressing. CAVEAT: this is a reading of press coverage, not of Bumble's Indian creative, and no India-specific Bumble fatigue campaign was found.
