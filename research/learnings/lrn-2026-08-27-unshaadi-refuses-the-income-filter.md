## Claim

A competitor refuses the income filter its own customers ask for, and reframes the request onto ego and role comfort rather than money — a formulation that may survive compliance rule 1 where the request itself does not

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Unshaadi's founder states on camera that successful women registering ask to be introduced only to men earning the same or more, that Unshaadi tells them 'no, no, we can't have that as a filter', and that the women then reframe it themselves: 'it is not a wealth filter, it's not a money filter — they fear that a man who earns much less than them will have ego issues, and they have experienced it in the past.' That is a claim about relationship dynamics and role comfort, not earnings. rules/compliance.md #1 bans money and provider-energy language; lrn-2026-08-26-knot-sells-income-verification records Knot selling income verification outright as the contrast case; lrn-2026-08-26-external-evidence-for-provider-ban records an Aisle user objecting to a salary question. Unshaadi is a third position between those two and the only one that both refuses the filter and discusses the refusal publicly. Whether the ego/dynamics reframe actually clears compliance #1 is a judgement rules/ has not made and this learning does not make for it.
