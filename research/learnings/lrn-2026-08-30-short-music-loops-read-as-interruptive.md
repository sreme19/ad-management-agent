## Claim

A music bed built by looping a single short clip is perceived as interruptive regardless of crossfade quality, and chaining several different generations is what removes the effect rather than changing the instrument

## Evidence

- (2026-08-30) The Riteangle cut needed roughly 17s of music under Acts 3 and 4 but Flow returned 4s clips. Crossfade-looping one 4s clip four times was rejected on listening as interruptive, and the objection persisted after instrumentation was changed from tabla percussion to sustained pads, identifying repetition rather than instrument choice as the cause. Chaining three different generations, one 10s and two 4s, crossfaded end to end, produced 16.6s with no repeated phrase. CONSEQUENCE FOR PLANNING: budget several music generations rather than one and vary them. The loop is more audible than any single instrument choice.
