## Claim

The three-panel storyboard strip generates natively at vertical 9:16 in Flow and each panel is usable, so the grid format survives the aspect ratio that held round 3 back

## Evidence

- (2026-08-29) Four three-panel vertical strips were generated in the 2026-08-29 session for the MOVE-ON lead funnel (Meher/bedroom, Kavya/library, Anaya/office, plus the master shape), and every one returned as a stacked vertical strip with clean gutters, legible panels and a readable left-to-right-then-down reading order. Round 3's grid was held back solely because 1280x720 landscape cannot crop to the 1080x1920 that Reels and Snap Story need, on the theory that a grid is composed for horizontal room to place panels side by side. Stacking the panels TOP TO BOTTOM instead of side by side removes that constraint entirely — the format's value was never the horizontal arrangement, it was seeing the beat progression before committing credits to motion. IMPORTANT CAVEAT ON RESOLUTION: every generation in this session returned 720x1280, not 1080x1920. So the format survives vertical COMPOSITION but the output still sits under Meta's recommended resolution for a paid asset, and whether a higher tier returns 1080x1920 was not tested. The strips were used as previews rather than as deliverables, which is the reframe that makes the resolution question secondary — round 3 tried to ship the grid as the ad itself.
