## Claim

Every audience split this account has ever reported worked only because the ad sets were named ad hoc, which means the metric got less reliable as the naming discipline improved

## Evidence

- (2026-08-26) The 6.9:1 male skew and the eight-women's-ad-sets finding that justified the current women's push were computed over ad sets with gender words in their names - the same non-conformance rules/naming.md exists to end. Those readings were sound when taken; the point is that the mechanism behind them was incidental, not designed, and it stops working from here on.
