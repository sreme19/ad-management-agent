## Claim

The one team in this organisation with real AI-video spend uses AI to test concepts and hires human creators to produce the winning asset

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) Briefing Session - Ads & Marketing Team, 28 Nov 2025. Avinash MB, describing the LexHive/DemandLane US legal lead-gen funnel where video is the primary format and AI video is used extensively: 'sometimes AI-generated content is not perfect and people can identify it as AI. They primarily use AI to test concepts and, once tested, they engage with actual creators from marketplaces to produce the final videos.' In the same session: 'Videos are performing better than images for them' and 'Most of the creatives that we use in our funnel are videos.' Supporting but weaker: a dashboard analysis referenced in Weekly Marketing Review 9 Mar 2026 'suggested focusing on real creators and doubling down on a real story angle' - the same meeting establishes that read is not trustworthy, because the real-vs-AI designation is inferred from ad names and Aditi Shinde 'estimated that the naming is about 80% accurate but the margin of error makes the data unusable for analysis.' Caveats: this is a different business (US personal-injury/mass-tort lead gen, English-language, far larger budgets), the marketplace creator cost is never stated, and no controlled AI-vs-human test is described anywhere in the corpus.
