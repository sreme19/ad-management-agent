## Claim

The call-centre follow-up channel is blocked on a company that is not yet registered, so phone capture currently has no lawful consumption path and cannot be the reason to add a phone field

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Operator stated in session on 2026-08-27 that a call-centre operation requires registering a company, that it is not registered, and that it is doable and something he will get to (note-2026-08-27-womens-funnel-matrix). This makes call-centre a sequencing constraint rather than a rejected option, but it also means a phone number captured today would sit unused: the entity that would call it does not exist, and India's outbound-calling regime attaches to a registered entity. It bears directly on q-2026-08-26-lead-capture-conversion, which asks whether a phone or email step on /get/w converts or just adds friction — a phone field is the higher-friction of the two and the one whose consumer is missing, so email is the field to test first on that page. Review when the registration status changes.
