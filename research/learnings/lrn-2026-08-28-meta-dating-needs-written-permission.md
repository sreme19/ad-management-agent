## Claim

Meta requires prior written permission before any dating ad may run, and nothing in rules/ records that requirement

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) Meta Advertising Standards, Restricted Goods and Services > Dating Ads (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/dating-ads/), page states last updated 25 Jun 2026, read 28 Aug 2026: 'Ads for dating services are only allowed with prior written permission.' An application form must be submitted and approved before running dating ads. The same standard requires all online dating ads to target 18+, and prohibits dating based on monetary or financial transactions, offers to facilitate affairs, 'connections with fictitious groups or individuals', and sexually suggestive emphasis. Snap by contrast restricts the dating category (18+ age-gating, content rules, and a blocked-country list that includes Pakistan but not India) without requiring prior written permission. As of 28 Aug 2026 rules/compliance.md contains no mention of written permission, prior approval or authorisation for the dating category - grep for 'written permission|prior permission|authoriz' returns only a Snap 18+ line - while rec-2026-08-27-moveon-w2530-meta is live on Meta. Not verified: whether the permission was in fact obtained out-of-band, and whether India carries any country-specific variation in the dating permission process.
