## Claim

Co-branding with a non-dating consumer brand is the default unit of dating-category marketing in India, not an occasional stunt

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Observed across four separate dating brands in two days: Bumble x HYROX (fitness event) and Bumble x inde wild (beauty); Tinder x Airtel (telecom), x Astrotalk (astrology), x Dunkin (food) and x Fantastik; Shaadi.com x MARS Cosmetics (the OOH billboard in note-2026-08-27-shaadi-blush-ooh-and-rakhi). Of 13 Bumble/Tinder posts observed, almost none is a product ad — one feature mention and one offer across the whole set. The partner brings an audience and a physical occasion the dating brand does not have. Riteangle has no partnership motion at all and rules/ contains no guidance on one. Caveat: no cost, reach or conversion data for any of these, and several are clearly agency-scale budgets.
