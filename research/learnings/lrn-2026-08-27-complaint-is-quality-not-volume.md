## Claim

Across every creator observed, the woman's complaint about dating is about the quality of what reaches her, never about being overwhelmed by choice

## Evidence

Not one of the five describes too many options to sort. rules/targeting.md persona #2 FLOODED-WOMAN describes exactly that, and campaigns/women-1822-casual-lpv spends against it under the hook slug FOURTEEN-SUITORS.
