## Claim

A competitor runs its entire content operation on other people's material plus a question, at effectively zero production cost, and its posts that ask something outperform its posts that state something

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Six Unshaadi posts observed. Sourced from inbound DMs or reposted with credit from @lisabilyeu, @shutterbonsai, @freeda_en, @theauntyeve — almost nothing is made in-house. Their highest post anywhere in this session, 73,800 likes, is a photograph someone else took in Los Angeles of a handwritten whiteboard listing nine marriage lessons, captioned 'Which points of this list do you agree with? What points would you add?'. The couple-interview repost took 5,341 likes and 984 comments, a 0.18 comment ratio an order of magnitude above any creator post observed, on a caption that pre-legitimises both sides of the argument. Their DM post announces the mechanic outright: 'I get a LOTTT of you DMing me... Would you like me to start sharing them here?' The two posts in the set with no question in the caption took 523 and 33 likes. Caveat: the top post is also the most universally shareable and least India-specific item in the set, so shareability alone could explain it; six posts spanning Sep 2025 to Mar 2026 is not a controlled comparison; and none of this says anything about registrations.
- (2026-08-28) **supported**: (2026-08-28) A second, longer-running instance of the same engine, outside the dating category and outside brand marketing entirely. @induviduality has run reader-submitted dating and sex stories, illustrated and posted under named project hashtags (#cockadoodle, #NotesToMyLovers, #Girlisthan), across posts dated 2018 to 2025 - eight years - at 800-1,700 likes per carousel with nothing produced in-house except the drawing. Unshaadi curates other people's public posts; this curates private accounts given directly to the creator, which is a stronger version of the same move. Caveat: seven-year span, no follower count, no rate, and the submission mechanic is read off captions rather than independently sourced. See note-2026-08-28-induviduality-crowdsourced.
