## Claim

Truecaller's Indian app gender split is not publicly established, and the ~60/40 male figure that surfaces in search is Similarweb's truecaller.com website traffic rather than the 350M+ Indian app user base — so it must not be used to price this channel.

## Evidence

- (2026-08-27) Searched for an India-specific app split on 2026-08-27 and found none; the only figure available is a website-traffic panel measuring a different population. Recorded as low deliberately: the decision it would drive (does Truecaller supply the already-oversupplied male side, per destinations.yaml's 98% male submissions) is too load-bearing to rest on a proxy.
