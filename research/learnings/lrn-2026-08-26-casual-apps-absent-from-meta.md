## Claim

All four Western casual/affair-adjacent apps have no discoverable Instagram presence, which reads as platform policy rather than choice

## Evidence

- (2026-08-26) Seeking, SDM Dating, Ashley Madison and Sugar Book returned no official account under any search variant; 7 of 24 competitors overall had none, but all four affair-adjacent apps are in that group. Consistent with Meta's community standards on infidelity-coded content, so it is a category constraint on the channel, not a research gap.
