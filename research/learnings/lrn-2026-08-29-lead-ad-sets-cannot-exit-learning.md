## Claim

Meta's learning phase needs roughly 50 conversion events per ad set per seven days, which a Rs 300/day lead campaign in India cannot reach, so the MOVE-ON lead ad sets will never exit learning

## Evidence

- (2026-08-29) Indian media-buying benchmark sources state Meta requires ~50 conversion events per ad set within a seven-day window to exit the learning phase, and that a low budget prevents this, producing unstable costs and poor delivery. Those sources recommend Rs 200-500/day for awareness or engagement testing but Rs 1,000/day or more specifically for LEAD or sales testing. The MOVE-ON funnel is set at Rs 300/day per ad set across two ad sets. At Rs 2,100 per ad set per week, reaching 50 submitted leads would require a cost per lead near Rs 42, against budget.md's own recorded women's benchmark of ~Rs 200/signup. So the ad sets will sit in learning for the whole window. This sharpens rather than contradicts budget.md, which already says a Rs 300 read is directional and that inconclusive is the expected verdict — it names the mechanism and puts a number on it, and it says the optimisation objective specifically will not converge. CAVEAT: every source is an agency or vendor blog with a commercial interest in recommending larger budgets, and the 50-event figure was not verified against Meta's own documentation in this pass.
