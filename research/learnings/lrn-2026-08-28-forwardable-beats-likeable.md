## Claim

A post built to be sent to one named person shares at 0.4-1.75 of its like count; a brand-template post shares at 0.03-0.06, and the gap holds inside the same account

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) (2026-08-28) newme.asia, four posts, same account: 'ask your sibling what's your vibe' 1,154 shares on 2,895 likes (0.40, caption instructs 'Share it with them'); the @hitakkshi red-flag creative 1,207 on 2,457 (0.49) with no instruction at all; 'wtf is denim' 1,822 on 18.1K (0.10); the produced Tomato Red carousel 317 on 2,028 (0.16). nykaafashion, six posts, same account: the repeating 'WHAT'S NEW on NYKAA FASHION - <brand>' template sits at 3-7 shares on 90-177 likes (0.028-0.056) every time, and its one untemplated diary line, 'back to shopping because healing was getting expensive', takes 57 on 576 (0.099). durex.india's SLIDERMAN film-title parody is the extreme: 65.8K shares on 37.6K likes, ratio 1.75. induviduality's ticketed-event poster, an actual invitation, shares at 0.65.
