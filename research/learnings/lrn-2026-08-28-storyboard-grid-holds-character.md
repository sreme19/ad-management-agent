## Claim

A storyboard-grid video format holds character consistency across a 10s spot where continuous-shot generation does not, because the model supplies only pans, reveals and re-crops between pre-composed panels rather than inventing motion.

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) One Google Flow generation, 2026-08-28, logged as creatives/_bakeoff/round-03-video-flow. 1280x720, 24fps, 10.005s. The same woman - coral blazer, bob - holds across roughly eight panels from street exterior through isolated reaction shots to a cafe two-shot, and a second character (the man) holds in the two-shot as well. The riteangle wordmark, heart mark and tagline all render legibly, which continuous AI video usually mangles. This is the same control-ladder principle as rungs 2-3 in note-2026-08-28-two-ai-video-tutorials: panels function as pre-approved stills, so temporal artifacts get no runway to accumulate. Confidence is low deliberately - n=1, nobody recorded whether a character reference was supplied or whether Flow invented her, and one frame at ~2.5s is a strained asymmetric grimace that reads as a blend artifact. The format did not advance: at 1280x720 landscape it cannot be cropped to the 1080x1920 that Snap Story and Meta Reels need, because the grid is composed for horizontal room.
