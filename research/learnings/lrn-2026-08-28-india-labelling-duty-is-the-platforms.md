## Claim

Under India's in-force IT Amendment Rules 2026 the duty to label AI content sits on the platform, and the advertiser's only direct duty is to declare truthfully when asked

## Evidence

- (2026-08-28) IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules 2026, G.S.R. 120(E), notified 10 Feb 2026 and in force 20 Feb 2026. Rule 2(1)(wa) defines synthetically generated information as information 'artificially created, generated, modified, or altered using a computer resource in a manner that appears real, authentic, or true', with carve-outs for routine editing such as cropping and colour correction - so a fully generated photorealistic person is SGI, a colour-graded real photo is not. Rule 3(3) requires SGI to be 'prominently label[led] through visual means where it is easily noticeable' with 'permanent metadata or other provenance mechanisms', and bars intermediaries from enabling removal of the label. Those duties bind intermediaries and significant social media intermediaries, not advertisers. Rule 4(1A) requires SSMIs to collect an SGI declaration at upload; the advertiser's exposure is a false declaration, which risks account suspension and IT Act penalties, with no SGI-specific fine schedule for advertisers found. The October 2025 draft's 10-percent-of-surface-area watermark did NOT survive into the final rules - the standard is qualitative. Two live caveats: a further draft circulated 21 Apr 2026 would require a label displayed continuously throughout visual content, consultation closed 7 May 2026 and it was not finalised as of 14 May 2026, status after that unverified and it would bite video hardest; and all of this was read from law-firm commentary (Khaitan, Argus, Freshfields, S S Rana) because the MeitY gazette PDF returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch. Meta separately mandates advertiser AI disclosure only for social-issue, electoral and political ads, and its self-disclosure duty covers photorealistic video and realistic audio rather than stills, which are handled by automatic metadata detection.
