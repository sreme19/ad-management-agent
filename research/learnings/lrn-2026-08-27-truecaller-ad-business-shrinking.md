## Claim

Truecaller's advertising business is contracting rather than growing — roughly 40% year-on-year revenue decline into Q2 2026 after losing about a third of its largest demand partner's traffic, with headcount cut and the company rotating toward recurring revenue — so inventory may be cheap but the self-serve product is unlikely to be well maintained.
