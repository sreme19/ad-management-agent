# @induviduality — Indu Harikumar's crowdsourced dating archive, and why it is unusable as-is

Eight screenshots handed over 28 Aug 2026, archived at
`creatives/_competitor-reference/_organic-creators/induviduality/`. Indu Harikumar is an
Indian illustrator who runs long-running participatory projects: readers send her their own dating,
sex and relationship experiences, she illustrates them and posts them as carousels under a project
hashtag. It is not a brand, not a competitor, and not sponsored content — which is exactly why it
belongs in the reference set. It is the clearest specimen available of an audience of Indian women
staying with long-form content about *exactly* Riteangle's subject matter, with no media spend behind
it.

## The reads

**1. The format is the product.** Almost none of this is Indu's own story. Readers send material in;
she illustrates it and gives it a project name (#cockadoodle, #NotesToMyLovers, #Girlisthan). It is
the same "curate and ask" engine `lrn-2026-08-27-curate-and-ask-beats-produce` recorded at Unshaadi,
running for **eight years** across at least four named series — and here the submissions are not
reposts of other people's public posts but private first-person accounts given directly to her.
Nobody in this repo's competitor set has anything comparable.

**2. The single most Riteangle-adjacent artefact in the whole reference folder is the 2020 four-panel**
— pretty, hot, financially independent, accomplished, *then why cannot he just love me?* That is the
`FLOODED-WOMAN` persona's actual interior monologue, drawn by someone who was sent it rather than
briefed on it. It sits at 809 likes with no brand attached.

**3. The event is the monetisation, and it sold out.** "Mapping Desire" is a Rs 500 ticketed 18+
performance in Pune, announced with a poster and RSVP-in-bio, and by the time the post was captured
the caption had been edited to "Registrations closed." Its 85 shares on 131 likes (0.65) is the
signature of an invitation being forwarded, not content being liked. An audience built on free
participatory storytelling converts to paid attendance offline.

## Why almost none of it can be touched

Four of the eight posts are sexually explicit in a way that is not borderline. `rules/compliance.md`
governs Riteangle's own assets and says nothing about who may be studied, but there is no version of a
co-branded post, a repost, a creative reference, or a paid partnership with this account that survives
either Meta's dating-vertical scrutiny (`lrn-2026-08-28-meta-dating-needs-written-permission`) or the
Apple 1.1.4 history that shaped the compliance file in the first place. **This account is a lesson
about a mechanic, not a shortlist entry for creator partnership**, and it should not be forwarded to
anyone as a partnership candidate without that sentence attached.

The transferable mechanic is narrow and specific: *ask your own users for the story, draw it, give the
series a name, and let the audience recognise themselves in someone else's words.* The two posts here
that carry no explicit content — the four-panel and the café-boundaries comic — are the ones that
demonstrate it cleanly, and they are the two that read most like a Riteangle ad already.

## Limits

Eight screenshots from one capture session, spanning 2018 to 2025 — seven years, so the like counts
are not comparable to each other and none of them is recent. No follower count was captured, so none
of this is a rate. No comment threads were read. The claim that readers submit the material is read
off the captions and hashtags visible in these screenshots plus the projects' own framing, not from
any independent source. Whether Indu Harikumar has ever taken brand work at all is unknown and was not
checked.
