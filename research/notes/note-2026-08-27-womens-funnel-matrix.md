# The women's-leads funnel matrix — operator direction, 27 Aug 2026

Spoken direction from Sree in session, 27 Aug 2026. Recorded because it widens the option
space every later mode is allowed to search, and because it states two hard constraints
(company registration, prior lead-form attempt) that exist nowhere else in this repo.

## What was said

Near-verbatim, lightly de-filled:

> When it comes to cracking the women leads, we have so far been just looking at image ads
> with some text on it, taking them as traffic into the landing page we have, and hopefully
> from there aiming for an app download. That's what we are aiming at, right?
>
> So I think we should explore other alternative angles like carousel ads, video ads, as it
> captures the leads themselves on the ad platform — which has been tried in the past.
> Emails that can be captured on the landing page, for instance, that's another idea.
>
> There's also another idea where if we can get a direct phone number, let's say, and email,
> we can do like a call-centre operation. We can start calling them, for instance. So that's
> an idea that I also want to try as well. WhatsApp marketing, you know?
>
> So I think these are the different strategies that we can mix and match. So keep this
> entire matrix in front of you. Let's start with, obviously, the least friction and the
> thing that can be easily launched. I need to still figure out the other pieces, which I
> will eventually do. Like, for example, to do a call-centre operation, I need to register a
> company and all that, which is not registered, but it's doable, right? So let's not shy
> away from all different kinds of strategies that can be deployed here. So put this as a
> note when it comes to the research that we have.

## The matrix as stated

Three axes, which the operator explicitly wants treated as mix-and-match rather than as one
fixed funnel:

| axis | what is running today | what was named as alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| ad format | static image with text overlay | carousel, video |
| capture point | none — landing page then Play Store | on-platform lead form (ad platform captures it), email capture on the landing page, phone + email capture |
| follow-up channel | none — the app install is the terminal event | call centre (outbound voice), WhatsApp marketing |

## Constraints the operator stated as fact

- **The call-centre option is blocked on a legal entity that does not exist.** A company has
  to be registered first. Described as not registered, doable, and something he will get to —
  a sequencing constraint, not a refusal.
- **On-platform lead capture "has been tried in the past."** Stated as prior history, not as
  a new idea.
- **The goal is women's leads.** The app download is described as what the current funnel
  aims at, not as the thing being optimised for in principle.

## Standing instruction

Keep the whole matrix in view when proposing. Sequence by launch friction, least first.
Do not narrow to the currently-running combination, and do not drop an option because its
prerequisites are not in place yet.
