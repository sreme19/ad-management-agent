## What is consistent across all three

The product claim is always the same and it is always about the **pool**, never about sorting,
effort, or volume of matches:

- "50:50 men-to-women ratio, **guaranteed**" — stated in two of three captions
- "Equality doesn't mean more choices. It means better choices."
- "compatibility-based matching"
- "DATE WITHOUT SURPRISES"

There is no shortlist, no ranking, no AI-does-the-work claim anywhere in this set. The promise is
that the population on the other side is balanced and pre-screened, and that what you meet will not
surprise you. Creative B extends it to a soft-landing promise — if the romance fails you still get a
friend — which converts a failed match from a loss into an outcome.
