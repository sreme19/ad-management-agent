downloaded and machine-transcribed locally (Whisper large-v3-turbo). **Transcript reliability differs
per reel and is stated inline — one of the three is not quotable.** Opening frames saved to
`creatives/_competitor-reference/_organic-creators/`.

creator-partnership scouting, not as competitor creative.

## The pattern across all three

Engagement, against the brand accounts observed the same day:

| Post | Production | Likes |
|---|---|---|
| @anerithakkar_ (bedroom, phone) | none | 5,499 |
| @malikkaadvani (bedroom, phone) | none | 3,229 |
| @wingwoman.sim (lav mic, banner) | light | 1,657 |
| shaadi.com Raksha Bandhan (studio) | full | 31 |
| VLNCY "better choices" (shot campaign) | full | 17 |
| VLNCY Friendship Day reel (stock/generated) | full | 4 |
| VLNCY World Cup (shot campaign) | full | 3 |

Follower counts differ and none of this is normalised, so this is not an engagement-rate claim. What
it shows is cruder and still useful: on the same platform, in the same category, in the same week,
the three posts that travelled were shot on phones in bedrooms by women talking to camera, and the
four that did not were produced brand assets.

**On the complaint itself.** Molly's is not a volume complaint and not a scarcity complaint — it is
about the *quality of the attention received*. The line is mass-produced; she is one of many
recipients of the same copy-paste effort. Aneri's, as far as it can be read, is about mismatch across
axes. wingwoman.sim's implicit criteria are fidelity, routine, and legibility.

None of the three is describing being overwhelmed by choice. Two of the three are describing
attention that is plentiful but worthless, which is a different thing from either a flood of options
or a desert — and it is closer to the beat-1 line already written in
`creatives/fourteen-suitors-w1822/prompts.md` ("Stop scrolling through guys who just want attention")
than to the `FOURTEEN-SUITORS` slug that beat sits under.
