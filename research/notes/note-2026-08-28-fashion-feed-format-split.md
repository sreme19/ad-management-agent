# Indian fashion/retail Instagram, 12 posts — what the women's feed actually rewards

Screenshots handed over 28 Aug 2026 (phone captures, timestamps 12:43–12:49). None of these accounts
is a dating competitor. They were brought in because they sell to the same person Riteangle's
`FLOODED-WOMAN` and `CASUAL-SELECTIVE` lanes are aimed at — Indian women 18–30 on Instagram — and
because a category with a real product to photograph shows the format split more cleanly than dating
does. Archived under `creatives/_competitor-reference/_adjacent-category/`; the NOT-SHIPPABLE rule in
that folder's README governs all of it.

Three things are visible in that table and none of them is about clothes.

1. **The only post that names an emotion instead of a product is the one with the highest comment
   count in the set** (148 on the red-flag creative, against 12 on the account's 18.1K-like winner).
   The five captions are not a slogan; they are five separate things a viewer might have said out loud
   this month, and the format lets her pick one. It is an ad for a knitted co-ord set that reads as a
   post about a man.
2. **Two posts were built to be forwarded to a named person, and they share at 0.40–0.49 — four to
   five times the account's own product posts.** The sibling post spells the instruction out in the
   caption ("Share it with them"). The red-flag post doesn't have to.
3. **The comment-keyword funnel is running here too.** "Comment 'Colours'" against 89 comments is the
   same mechanic recorded at `lrn-2026-08-27-comment-keyword-funnel-captures-leads` from
   @malaikaneri, now seen on a verified retail brand rather than a matchmaker — but at a 0.044
   comment-to-like ratio, far below malaikaneri's 0.71. Worth noting that the mechanic does not
   automatically produce the ratio.

The account runs a repeating branded template — "WHAT'S NEW on NYKAA FASHION — <brand>" — across at
least three posts and it sits at 90–177 likes and 3–7 shares every time. The one post in the set with
no template, no partner logo and no product name in the type outperforms all of them by 3–6× and
shares at 8–19× their rate. Its line is a joke about the cost of therapy, and the Nykaa boxes are
just the set dressing.

- **A reel whose attribution line reads "AI content"** — not a caption, not a hashtag, the
  platform-rendered label where the audio credit normally sits. A mainstream Indian retailer is
  shipping AI-generated video creative into feed with the label attached and nothing about the post
  is otherwise marked. Directly relevant to

## Two paid ads caught in the same scroll

- **@lamastoreindia** — in-feed Ad, "Shop now" bar. A real woman in front of a clothing rack, holding
  a printed dress up to camera, socks on, no set, phone-grade light. Nothing about it is produced.
  It is the retail version of the phone-shot talking-head that
  `lrn-2026-08-26-brand-voice-organic-is-dead` records as the only organic format that works,
  running as paid.
- **@gametheoryindia** (kids' skating academy) — in-feed Ad, "Book now". Headline *"Their **wobbles**
  are working"* with "wobbles" set in a blue script against a black sans, then "Active & safe coaching
  for stronger, more confident skaters", then **₹2,384 per month, upto 40% off**. 1 like, 16 comments.
  Out of category entirely, but it is a clean specimen of the price-and-discount ad format: the
  emotional headline earns the read, the number closes it, and the engagement is irrelevant because
  the objective is a booking. Included because Riteangle has no price to put in that slot, which is
  itself worth knowing when copying this shape.

## Limits

Single screenshots, one capture session, no follower counts recorded for any of these accounts, so
nothing here is an engagement *rate* and cross-account comparisons are not normalised. Within-account
comparisons (newme's four, nykaafashion's six) are the load-bearing part. Reposts and shares are as
Instagram displayed them at capture time. No comment threads were read. Whether any of these posts had
paid amplification behind it is unknowable from the surface — the two marked "Ad" are the only ones
known to be paid, and the 18.1K and 576 figures could both be boosted numbers for all this shows.
