## 1. Dating-app fatigue — is the wedge occupied?

**Forbes Health survey (via search result summary):** a 2024 survey of 1,000 Americans found 79% of
Gen Z users experience burnout from dating apps (Tinder, Hinge, Bumble). Women report it more than
men: **80% vs 74%.** SAMPLE IS AMERICAN, not Indian. n=1,000 for the whole survey, not for the
gender split.
Source: https://www.forbes.com/health/dating/dating-app-fatigue/ (via search summary; the Forbes page
itself was not fetched)

**Bumble is publicly repositioning onto fatigue.** Search summary reports Bumble "embracing
AI-powered algorithms to re-engage those who crave an experience that feels less overwhelming and
more purposeful", and "removing its swipe feature". Founder Whitney Wolfe Herd told Fortune that
dating apps are "rooted in rejection and judgement" and that this is "not healthy", vowing to bring
"more joy and satisfaction".
Sources: https://fortune.com/2024/04/30/bumble-app-relaunch-rebrand-women-first-move-ceo-lidiane-jones
https://theweek.com/culture-life/personal-technology/how-dating-apps-are-fighting-swipe-fatigue

**Bumble's 2024 global rebrand** launched in 10+ countries with the line "We've changed so you don't
have to", plus the Opening Moves feature. Covered by Campaign India, so it reached the Indian trade
press. YouGov reported brand-health struggles through 2024-25 following the rebrand and the
"celibacy" ad controversy.
Sources: https://www.campaignindia.in/article/bumble-unveils-new-global-campaign-visual-identity/495830
https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/51733-from-rebrand-to-controversy-bumbles-brand-health-struggles-in-2024-2025

**No India-specific Bumble or Tinder fatigue campaign was found** in this pass. Searched explicitly
for one; the results returned global campaigns and US survey coverage only.

**TrueBondr blog, "Dating App Fatigue — How Indian Women Protect Mental Health While Dating Online
2026".** Fetched and read. IT CONTAINS NO STATISTICS, no survey numbers, no sample sizes and cites no
studies. It is a branded content-marketing post by a dating app promoting itself. Its stated
complaints are: (1) women receive higher message VOLUME than men, creating screening fatigue;
(2) safety cognitive load — verifying authenticity, assessing meeting safety; (3) emotional labour,
"constant first impressions", conversations feeling "like a job". No direct quotes from Indian women;
the article is third-person throughout and the "signs of fatigue" appear to be the author's synthesis.
Source: https://www.truebondr.in/blog/dating-app-fatigue-how-indian-women-protect-mental-health-while-dating-online-2026

## 2. Script — romanised Hindi vs Devanagari

Search summary of an academic source (Signs and Society, University of Chicago Press / Hamilton
College PDF, "Language, Script, and Advertising in India's Hindi Belt: Institutional Voices in Flux")
plus language blogs:

- **Roman script (Hinglish) dominates among youth on Instagram, WhatsApp and Twitter.** Devanagari
  appears more in formal posts, news, and older demographics.
- Reasons given: keyboard/typing friction for Devanagari on phones; no keyboard switching; easier
  code-mixing of Hindi with English.
- **The advertising implication, stated directly:** "An ad written almost entirely in Devanagari
  signals to the viewer that it is targeting a completely different audience, typically in Hindi
  heartland regions rather than urban youth."
Sources: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/706035
https://people.hamilton.edu/documents/706035.pdf
https://www.jnanamrit.com/2025/08/08/the-growing-preference-for-roman-script-in-writing-hindi/
https://trykaiwa.com/blog/hindi-social-media-language-guide

## 3. Pan-India delivery at a low daily budget

Search summaries of Indian media-buying benchmark pages (not Meta's own documentation):

- **CPM varies sharply by geography within India:** "Running ads to users in South Delhi or South
  Mumbai yields much higher CPMs compared to targeting Tier-3 cities in regions like Bihar or Uttar
  Pradesh."
- **Meta's learning phase requires ~50 conversion events per ad set within a 7-day window.** A low
  budget "prevents campaigns from exiting the learning phase, resulting in unstable costs and poor
  ad delivery."
- Practical recommendations quoted: Rs 200-500/day for awareness or engagement testing;
  **Rs 1,000/day or more for LEAD or sales testing.**
- India Facebook CPM quoted as roughly Rs 50-400.
Sources: https://upgrowth.in/facebook-advertising-pricing/
https://techeasify.com/minimum-budget-for-facebook-ads-in-india/
https://paidmediaworld.com/cost-of-facebook-ads-india-2026/
https://www.vgraple.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-india-2026/

CAVEAT ON THIS SECTION: every source is an agency/vendor blog with an interest in recommending
higher budgets. The 50-events-per-7-days figure is widely repeated and matches Meta's published
learning-phase guidance, but was not verified against Meta's own documentation in this pass.
