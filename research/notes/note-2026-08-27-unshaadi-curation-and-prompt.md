# Unshaadi, part two: the account is a curation-and-prompt machine

Five more Unshaadi posts observed 27 Aug 2026, extending
`note-2026-08-27-unshaadi-founder-as-channel`. One reel was pulled from its link and transcribed
locally; four were screenshots pasted in conversation with no file saved, so this text is their only
record. Reel frame at `creatives/_competitor-reference/unshaadi/shutterbonsai-couple-interview.jpg`.

**The finding that ties these together: Unshaadi barely makes anything.** Every post below except the
DM screenshot is someone else's content, reposted with credit and a question bolted on. Credits
observed: `@shutterbonsai`, `@lisabilyeu`, `@freeda_en`, and `@theauntyeve` (via the founder's
account in the earlier note). Recurring hashtags: **#unshaaditalks**, **#meetyourlobster**.

like me to start sharing them here?** / 2. Have you been in a comparable situation? As the girl, or
the guy? Share your experience! / 3. What would you say to her? / Let's talk."
Hashtags: #unshaaditalks #unshaadi #meetyourlobster

Comments: sakkunama, sarcastically — "You can ask him to grow the food in land by himself because
when you cook yourself you like to cook only that food which is grown by your partner's own hands.
If he is able to grow the food you will be happily cooking the food at home. 😊" — **92 likes**;

**This post is the origin story of the format.** It announces the mechanic out loud: readers DM their
situations, Unshaadi publishes them, the comments do the work. The confession post in the earlier
note ("My husband's best friend kissed me at a party three months ago", 5,039 likes) is this pipeline
running at scale — the sourcing is free and inbound, and the audience supplies both the raw material
and the response.

Note the substance is the same axis as `note-2026-08-27-aseem-the-broken-trade`: two IIM-C
postgraduates in similar roles, and the housework still lands on her. She names it precisely — not
the cooking, the expectation and the indifference.

## The spread, and what separates top from bottom

| Post | Source | Prompt in caption? | Likes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 lessons whiteboard | @lisabilyeu (US) | Yes — "which points do you agree with?" | **73,800** |
| Couple interview | @shutterbonsai | Yes — "let's talk, zero judgement" | 5,341 |
| Confession ("husband's best friend") | inbound DM | Yes — "?" | 5,039 |
| DM: the kitchen | inbound DM | Yes — three numbered questions | 599 |
| Holi reel | unattributed footage | No | 523 |
| Handsome / Also handsome | @freeda_en (EU) | No | 33 |

Every post above 5,000 carries an explicit invitation to argue. The two at the bottom are statements.
The correlation is not proof — the top post is also the most universally shareable and least
India-specific thing in the set, and shareability alone could explain 73.8K — but the pattern holds
across six posts and it is the variable Unshaadi themselves control.

**The operating model, stated plainly:** source free (inbound DMs, other people's reposts with
credit), add a question, let the comment section generate the content. Production cost is
approximately zero. `lrn-2026-08-27-the-penalty-is-product-not-production` already found the penalty
attaches to product-talk rather than production value; this is the positive form of the same finding
— a competitor that has stopped making product content altogether and is winning attention with
other people's material plus a question mark.

## Limits

No follower count for `unshaadi` was captured, so none of these are rates, and the dates span
Sep 2025 to Mar 2026 across different algorithm conditions. Four of the six are single frames of
carousels. Comment threads were read only as far as each screenshot showed. Nothing here indicates
whether any of this converts to registrations — Unshaadi's engagement says nothing about its
membership, and `lrn-2026-08-27-the-penalty-is-product-not-production` notes the product post drew
argument at a far higher *rate* on far less reach, which is the counter-case.
