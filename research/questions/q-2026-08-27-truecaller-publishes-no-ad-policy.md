## Question

Does Truecaller permit dating-app advertising at all, and where is the policy written — is there any published advertiser content policy or restricted-category list?

## Why it matters

Searched on 2026-08-27 and found none. Meta and Google both publish explicit dating-ad policies that creative can be designed against; Truecaller appears to publish nothing, so category eligibility is only discoverable by submitting an ad for review. That means creative could clear this repo's own compliance gate, clear QA, and still be rejected against an unpublished rule — the whole creatives/_bakeoff spend on a Truecaller-format asset is exposed to it. Worth asking Truecaller directly at adsmanager@truecaller.com before any creative is cut for this channel.
