## Question

What sample size and date range produced the 98% male lead-form split, and can the number be recovered from Snap's reporting now?

## Why it matters

Three normative files — rules/creative-generation.md, rules/destinations.yaml and SPEC.md — rest on this figure, and none of them records an n or a window. It decides whether on-platform lead forms are a burned channel or an underpowered test, which is the difference between dropping that column of the funnel matrix and re-running it now that the POV rule and /get/w both exist. It is also the one number in this repo that is load-bearing and unsourced.
