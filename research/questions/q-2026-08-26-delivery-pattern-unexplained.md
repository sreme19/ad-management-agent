## Question

What explains WOMEN_18-22_CASUAL_LPV's delivery pattern - stuck in learning phase, 8.1x frequency, the daily cap consumed in about ninety minutes - now that the missing pixel has been ruled out?

## Why it matters

All three were attributed to the pixel and that attribution was wrong. An unexplained delivery pattern does not stay unexplained; it gets attached to whatever hypothesis is nearest when the next weak read arrives. The Rs 300 campaign cap is the obvious candidate - a cap below budget.md's floor, spent fast against a broad pan-India audience, would plausibly produce all three - but that is a hypothesis and has not been checked. Whatever the answer, it decides whether this run can be read at all or has to be rerun uncapped.
