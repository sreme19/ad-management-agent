## Question

What does one MOVE-ON video clip actually cost — Flow credits and model tier — for a three-panel vertical storyboard strip, and separately for an 8-10 second animation from it? And does a higher tier return 1080x1920 rather than the 720x1280 every generation produced on 2026-08-29?

## Why it matters

The four-clip pipeline is now demonstrated end to end but has never been costed, across two rounds. Without a per-clip figure there is no way to say whether this is repeatable at ad volume or whether a four-scene film is affordable per variant, which is the question that decides if AI video is a testing tool or a production tool here. The resolution half matters independently: 720x1280 is under Meta's recommended spec for Reels and gets recompressed on delivery, so a paid asset is stacking compression on under-resolution. Both numbers are visible at generation time and cost nothing to record.
