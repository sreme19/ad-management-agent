## Question

Why is Meta's {{lead_id}} macro not resolving into ra_lead on the /get/w-apply arrival, and can it be made to?

## Why it matters

It is the only key that can join a Meta lead to her landing-page visit and install. Without it the daily lead report can never answer 'did these people convert', only 'the campaign produced N arrivals'. The cheatsheet made a real ra_lead a precondition for enabling the campaign and it was enabled without one.
