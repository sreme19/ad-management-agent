## Question

Which click-URL parameters can a Truecaller ad carry, does the platform expose ad-level and campaign-level macros at all, and does pocket-dating-coach normalise utm_source=truecaller into its traffic join?

## Why it matters

It decides both halves of a registry entry that cannot otherwise be written: networks.yaml requires a real ad_join_param and ad_set_join_param, and a DanAds-style booking tool may only accept one static click URL per creative, in which case ad-level attribution is a per-ad hardcoded slug rather than a macro. Separately, spend on a network pocket-dating-coach has never heard of cannot join to its traffic at all. Launching before both are answered repeats the 2026-08-21 incident by construction.
