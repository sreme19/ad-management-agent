## Question

Is there a permitted path to WhatsApp as a destination or follow-up channel, and what would it need in networks.yaml and destinations.yaml?

## Why it matters

WhatsApp is the only option in the operator's funnel matrix with no representation anywhere in the registries — it is neither a network nor a destination, and click-to-WhatsApp would run on Meta, where creation is 'none' and no credential exists. Before it can be costed as an idea we need to know whether it is a Meta ad destination, a post-capture messaging channel, or both, and whether a WhatsApp Business account exists. Until that is answered, any WhatsApp recommendation is unpriceable.
