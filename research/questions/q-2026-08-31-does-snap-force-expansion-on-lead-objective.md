## Question

Does Snap's own documentation say that a LEAD_FORM_SUBMISSIONS ad squad forces targeting expansion (SMART_TARGETING) on, and if so, can it be turned off at all - in the Marketing API, in Ads Manager, or neither? Secondary: if it can be turned off in the UI, does it stay off, or does Snap re-enable it after the squad starts delivering?

## Why it matters

This decides whether the women's Snap lead funnel is worth continuing in its current shape. lrn-2026-08-31-snap-forces-expansion-on-lead-squads observed expansion ON across every lead squad on the account despite the API being sent expansion=false, and lrn-2026-08-31-snap-women-targeted-leads-deliver-male recorded ~90 percent male delivery on three of exactly those squads. If expansion cannot be held off on a Snap lead objective, then rec-2026-08-29-moveon-lead-w1830-snap's central argument - that hard gender plus expansion off boxes the algorithm into women - is not available on this platform, and the women's lead test must either accept a male-drift tax as a permanent cost of the format on Snap, move to Meta where Advantage Audience is at least refusable, or drop the lead objective for the web-view funnel that DOES hold expansion off. Answering it is a documentation read plus one Ads Manager check, not an experiment.
