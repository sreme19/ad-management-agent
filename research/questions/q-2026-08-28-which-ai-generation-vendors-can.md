## Question

Which AI generation vendors can actually be paid from India with an Indian card or UPI, and which silently cannot?

## Why it matters

This was the one thing a full research pass could not settle for a single vendor. Every tool comparison assembled on 2026-08-28 - Veo, Kling, Seedance, Wan, MiniMax, Runway, Luma, Pika, Higgsfield - returned USD list prices and no confirmation of Indian card acceptance, UPI, or behaviour under RBI recurring-mandate rules. Runway publishes a Stripe-backed method list with no UPI and no India rail; Kling's prepaid API floor is a $700 lot; Higgsfield's own JS carries T1/T2/T3 plan-set keys and a with_localization flag, so India is served different prices than any blog quotes, and Forbes reported unspecified 'payment problems' there. A medium recommendation that cannot be paid for is not a recommendation. The cheapest way to answer it is to attempt one small real checkout per shortlisted vendor and record what happens.
