## Question

Does the storyboard-grid video format survive being generated at 1080x1920 vertical, and was the character in the round-3 Flow candidate referenced or invented? Also unrecorded at generation time: what it cost in Flow credits, and which model tier produced it (Veo 3.1, 3.1 Fast, or the free Omni Flash).

## Why it matters

The round-3 candidate scored 27.75/37.5 and was held back solely on aspect ratio - 1280x720 landscape cannot be cropped to the 1080x1920 that Snap Story and Meta Reels need, because the grid is composed for horizontal room to place panels side by side. So the entire value of the format finding rests on whether it survives vertical. If it does, it is the cheapest route we have to a 10s spot that holds a face. If it does not, the consistency result was an artifact of landscape composition and does not transfer. The referenced-or-invented question decides whether the finding is about Flow's reference adherence or about the format itself, and the cost question decides whether it is repeatable at ad volume.

## Answer (2026-08-29)

Answered by lrn-2026-08-29-vertical-strip-format-works: The three-panel storyboard strip generates natively at vertical 9:16 in Flow and each panel is usable, so the grid format survives the aspect ratio that held round 3 back
