## Question

Would a phone or email capture step on /get/w convert Snap traffic into contactable leads, or just add friction ahead of the Play Store button?

## Why it matters

It is the highest-leverage gap the landing-page research found, and the stated goal is women's leads rather than installs. Landing-page views are the current success metric precisely because taps and signups are not yet trustworthy, so the answer also decides what a good result even looks like.

**Update 2026-08-27 — the form is now built, and the question is unchanged.** The capture shipped on /get/w (WhatsApp or email, into `marketing_leads`). Building it does not answer this: the question was never whether we could capture a lead, it was whether capture converts Snap traffic or just adds friction ahead of the Play button. What has changed is that the answer is now measurable rather than hypothetical. It is answerable once the MOVE-ON-PROPER ad set has run, by comparing store-tap rate on /get/w against the pre-form baseline and against /get over the same window — if taps hold and leads arrive, the form is free; if taps fall by more than the leads are worth, it is not.

Note the earlier framing here — "the fix is a pocket-dating-coach route change, not an ad change - this repo can only recommend it" — is no longer true in practice. The change was made directly.
