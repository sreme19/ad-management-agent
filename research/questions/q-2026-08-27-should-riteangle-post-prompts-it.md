## Question

Should Riteangle post prompts it does not answer, and treat the comment thread as the deliverable rather than the copy?

## Why it matters

Unshaadi's brand post is a single confession line on a plain background captioned only '?', and it took 5,039 likes while two individual commenters took 1,441 and 1,514 likes each — roughly 30 percent of the post's own volume captured by one reply. The founder does the same in his caption: 'Guess if we accept these women in the pool or not?' Withholding the opinion is what generates the thread. Riteangle has no format like this, and two things block it as-is: rules/creative-style.md's objection-handling table has no row for hostility and rules/ has no comment-moderation policy for a live ad, which note-2026-08-27-three-women-creators-on-dating already flagged as an unhandled surface after the male-backlash cluster under the Hinge reel. A prompt format deliberately invites exactly that. This question decides whether the format is worth the moderation cost, and what a Riteangle prompt could ask that compliance would allow.
