## Question

Does India's IT Amendment Rules 2026 labelling duty for synthetically generated information bind Riteangle as the advertiser, or only the platform as intermediary?

## Why it matters

MeitY notified the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules 2026 on 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026, bringing synthetically generated information into due-diligence obligations with prominent labelling at upload and a bar on removing such labels or metadata. Every Riteangle asset is synthetic. But the research only reached law-firm commentary (Freshfields, S S Rana, RNA IP), never the gazette text, and the decisive question - whether the duty sits on the intermediary or on the advertiser uploading the creative - was not answered. It matters in both directions: if the duty binds us, rules/compliance.md is missing a legal requirement; and it cuts directly against rules/creative-generation.md section 4, which forbids AI labels of any kind in generated output. [master] Riteangle - marketing requirement.docx already says 'Label AI imagery... Creative that shows a portrait should not imply it is an untouched snapshot', and no rules/ file carries that clause. This is a lawyer question, not a research question.

## Answer (2026-08-28)

Answered by lrn-2026-08-28-india-labelling-duty-is-the-platforms: Under India's in-force IT Amendment Rules 2026 the duty to label AI content sits on the platform, and the advertiser's only direct duty is to declare truthfully when asked
