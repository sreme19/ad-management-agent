## Question

The MOVE-ON funnel now aims Hindi-first copy at a pan-India audience that will deliver largely outside the metros, while its creative casting is written around Bangalore rooftops, Indiranagar footpaths and cafe window tables. Which one moves — the imagery to a non-metro register, or the geo back to metros?

## Why it matters

It decides what gets generated in Google Flow. The round-05 casting names Bangalore in every prompt because Flow's backgrounds default Western, so re-aiming the imagery is a prompt-pack rewrite, not a crop. Getting this wrong means paying for impressions in one India and showing them a picture of a different one.
