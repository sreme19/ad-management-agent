## Question

On the Meta MOVE-ON ad's day-1 read, 4 of the 5 store-click taps come from a single US city (Altoona) on 5 US views, inside one 00:15 bucket before Indian delivery started, while 391 Indian views produced 1 tap. Are those 4 taps real users, an unfiltered scanner/preview fetch, or our own testing traffic — and separately, why does visitFunnel report tapped=2 while the ad-set leaderboard reports taps=5 for the same range?

## Why it matters

The 2026-09-02 verdict on this record turns on whether the tap rate is 1.26% or 0.26%. If a handful of non-Indian, non-human taps can pass the bot filter and dominate a small-sample tap count, every low-spend read on this account is distorted the same way, and the two disagreeing tap counts mean we do not currently know which number the verdict should quote.
