## Question

Does Riteangle have any route to a real, consented, first-person story from one of its own users - written, recorded or paraphrased - that could be published in an ad?

## Why it matters

lrn-2026-08-28-demand-shows-up-as-submission and lrn-2026-08-27-curate-and-ask-beats-produce both point at the same format, and compliance rules/compliance.md 6.2 blocks a synthetic person narrating a first-person Riteangle experience. A real consented story is the only version of that format that is shippable, and whether one can be obtained is a product/ops question, not a creative one.
